# CrestLine Financial Services — Employee Leave Policy

## Document Information

- Document ID: HR-POL-001
- Department: Human Resources
- Classification: Internal
- Intended Audience: All Employees
- Version: 1.0

## Purpose

This policy describes the leave available to employees of CrestLine Financial Services and explains the process for requesting and approving leave.

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees are entitled to 20 working days of annual leave per calendar year.

Employees should submit annual leave requests through the internal employee portal at least five working days before the proposed start date.

Leave requests are subject to manager approval and operational requirements.

## Sick Leave

Employees who are unable to work because of illness should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

For extended periods of absence, Human Resources may request appropriate supporting documentation.

## Emergency Leave

Emergency leave may be requested when an unexpected personal or family circumstance requires immediate attention.

Employees should notify their manager and Human Resources as soon as possible.

## Approval Process

1. Employee submits a leave request.
2. The request is reviewed by the employee's manager.
3. Approved requests are recorded in the employee portal.
4. Human Resources may review leave records for administrative purposes.

## Security Notice

This document does not contain employee-specific information, credentials, authentication tokens, or confidential customer information.

## Contact

Employees with questions about leave should contact the Human Resources department through the internal employee portal.
